ABOUT TECHFEST 2026

Established in 1998, Techfest IIT Bombay has grown to become Asia's Largest Science and Technology Festival. Hosting a footprint that spreads across dozens of universities, research labs, and private enterprises globally, Techfest represents a platform for the next generation of engineers.

From breaking the code of high-level secure algorithms to experiencing extreme robotic shockwaves inside our battle-box, Techfest encapsulates a futuristic legacy.
